# SELF-IMPROVE – Newsflash relevance ratings & improvements

Automated self-improvement pass. At the end of each run, review the previous newsflash file(s) in the News Log, read the user's per-item relevance ratings, find patterns, and record conclusions and concrete actions. Newest at the end. The entry format is defined in section 6.2 of `Newsflash SOP`.
