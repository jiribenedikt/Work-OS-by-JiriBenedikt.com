# Voice and Writing Conventions

Read only when drafting, editing, or reviewing text in the user's voice for other people, such as emails or reports.

## Language
Record the language(s) to write in and any regional conventions (spelling, date and number formats).

## Tone
Record the intended tone (for example formal, warm, direct) and how it shifts by audience.

## Style rules
Record sentence length, formatting habits, sign-offs, and words or punctuation to use or avoid.

## Approved examples
Keep short samples of the user's own writing that they consider on-voice.
