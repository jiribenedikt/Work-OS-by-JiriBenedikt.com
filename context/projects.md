# Projects and Ongoing Work

Read when working on a client engagement or initiative, recalling context, or recording decisions and progress. Date entries and label uncertain items.

## Active projects
Add one short entry per project: name, client or owner, goal, status, and last-updated date.

## Decisions log
Add one line per meaningful decision: date, decision, and the reason.

## Notes and open questions
Record context, risks, and unresolved questions that future work will need.
