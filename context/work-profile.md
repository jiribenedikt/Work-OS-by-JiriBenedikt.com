# Work Profile

Read when a task depends on the user's professional background, audience, positioning, or tools. Date changing entries and label uncertain ones as assumptions.

## Role and expertise
Record the user's role, seniority, and areas of expertise.

## Clients and audiences
Record who the user serves or writes for, and what those audiences care about.

## Positioning
Record how the user describes their value, offers, and differentiators.

## Platforms and content channels
Record where the user publishes or communicates (for example newsletter, social networks, website) and any channel-specific norms.
